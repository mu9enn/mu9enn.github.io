About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in a joint program between Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) and Shanghai AI Laboratory. I am deeply honored to be advised by Prof. Xiaosong Wang. I received my B.Eng. degree in Robotics Engineering from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC).

My current research focuses on developing AI agents for scientific discovery. Previously, I was an undergraduate researcher at UESTC HiLab supervised by Prof. Guotai Wang and Prof. Shaoting Zhang, where I explored fairness and fine-tuning methods for vision-language models (VLMs) in medical imaging.
